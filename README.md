## Overview

This repository contains the doctoral candidacy proposal, its supporting mathematical appendices, and the accompanying Wolfram Mathematica implementation for the research project **“Multiphoton Interference and Representation Theory in the Many-Photon Limit.”**

The research investigates quantitative correspondences among multiphoton interference with partial indistinguishability, classical multiport interferometry, and the semiclassical behavior of irreducible representations of \(U(M)\), in the limit of a large number of photons \(N\to\infty\) with a fixed number of optical modes \(M\).

The project is organized around three complementary connections: the relation between multiphoton probabilities and the classical map from relative phases to output intensities; the exact decomposition of multiphoton probabilities into irreducible representation sectors through Schur–Weyl duality; and the development of semiclassical approximations for representation matrix elements beyond the fully symmetric sector.

The mathematical framework combines exact finite-photon-number identities, numerical constructions, and conditional asymptotic results. Open problems include the contributions of stationary points and their interference, the behavior near caustics, and the relation between physical sectorial probabilities and the semiclassical geometry associated with irreducible representations.

## Repository Contents

The repository contains three principal files:

### 1. [PhD_Candidacy_Exam.pdf](PhD_Candidacy_Exam.pdf)

The main doctoral candidacy document presents the physical motivation, theoretical background, established results, research problem, specific objectives, methodology, and proposed contributions.

It develops the three correspondences underlying the research and distinguishes the results already obtained from the questions to be addressed during the doctoral project.

### 2. [Mathematical_Appendices.pdf](Mathematical_Appendices.pdf)

The supporting mathematical document contains nine appendices (A–I) providing detailed derivations and numerical illustrations of the theoretical framework.

These appendices cover the exact probabilistic formulation and Schur–Weyl reduction; spectrum–diagonal geometry and compatible unitary frames; construction and projection of irreducible representations; Gelfand–Tsetlin and isospin multiplicities; projected-state kernels and Gram matrices; physical sectorial density matrices and detection probabilities; stationary conditions and Hessians; and the derivation of the sectorial classical map.

The appendices explicitly distinguish exact mathematical identities, numerical evidence, and asymptotic statements requiring additional hypotheses.

### 3. [Multiphoton_Interference_Calculations.wl](Multiphoton_Interference_Calculations.wl)

The accompanying Wolfram Language code provides symbolic and numerical routines for the mathematical constructions developed in the candidacy document and its appendices, together with an interactive graphical interface for exploring the corresponding results and visualizations.

To run the program, download the `.wl` file, open it in **Wolfram Mathematica 14.5**, and evaluate the complete file. The interactive interface launches automatically, providing access to the computational tools and graphical representations.

## Computational Information

The numerical calculations reported in the mathematical appendices were performed using **Wolfram Mathematica 14.5** at machine precision. Numerical results complement the analytical derivations but do not replace mathematical proofs or the assumptions required for asymptotic statements.
